# V62 — Email Related Record Selector

Compose and Edit now include an explicit Related CRM record selector.

- Choose a Property, Deal, or Surplus case from the email form.
- Surplus options display the case number, Case Identifier parcel ID, and claimant.
- The selected record is validated and authorized on the server.
- Selecting a Surplus case makes its Case Identifier parcel and county available to merge fields.
- Contacts are not automatically mapped to a case because one contact can be linked to multiple parcels.

No database migration or frontend build is required.
