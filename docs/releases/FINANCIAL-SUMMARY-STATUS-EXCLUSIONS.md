# Financial summary status exclusions

Research, Bidding, and Archived properties no longer contribute to any Financials summary value.

- Portfolio Value, Total All-in, and Expected Profit: Owned, Actively Working, Marketing, and Under Contract.
- Actual Sales and Actual Profit: Owned, Actively Working, Marketing, Under Contract, and Sold.

Individual property financial records remain available for authorized users to configure and review; the exclusion applies to summary calculations.
