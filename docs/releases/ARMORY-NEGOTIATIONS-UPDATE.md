# Armory Negotiations update

This cumulative release adds a persistent negotiation-planning area inside Armory.

## Included

- Property-linked or standalone negotiation plans.
- Optional buyer Contact assignment.
- Draft, Active, Accepted, and Closed statuses.
- Asking price, all-in amount, and buyer-offer input.
- Fixed 100%–50% Core Price Ladder in 2.5% steps.
- Fixed 20% VVR / 40% Investor 1 / 40% Investor 2 projections.
- Signed below-break-even projections.
- Buyer offer percentage, profit, split projection, and closest-ladder highlighting.
- User-selected counter percentage with calculated price, profit, and split projection.
- Policies, validation, transaction service, audit events, factory, tests, and documentation.

## Bayberry acceptance example

- Asking price: $22,000
- All-in amount: $14,100
- Profit at ask: $7,900
- VVR at ask: $1,580
- Each investor at ask: $3,160
- Buyer offer: $16,900
- Buyer offer percentage: 76.8%
- Selected counter percentage: 77.5%
- Counter offer: $17,050

Negotiation calculations are advisory projections only. They do not create financial transactions or modify Property Financials.
